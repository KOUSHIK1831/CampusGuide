# CampusGuide demonstration knowledge base

This document contains fictitious example policies for demonstrations and unit tests.
It is NOT an official ICFAI University policy.

## Library
Students may borrow up to four library books at once. Loan periods are fourteen days.

## Academic advising
Students book academic advising sessions by emailing the academic advising desk.
Advising sessions take place on Wednesdays.

## Lost ID card
A lost student ID card should be reported to the campus administration desk.
Replacement cards are issued after identity verification.

## Campus cafeteria
The cafeteria serves lunch between 12:00 and 14:00 on weekdays.
